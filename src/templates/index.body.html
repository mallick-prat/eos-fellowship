The Fellowship

Eos Fellows is a close-knit community of exceptional student and early-career builders and aspiring investors.

Members get access to in-network opportunities (VC and startup internships, credits, introductions), mentorship from founders, investors, and operators, and a peer group that could be your lifelong friends and colleagues.

The person sitting next to you could become your co-founder, your first hire, or a lifelong friend; and, we are so excited to do this with the support of mentors, including the founders of Mercor, Applied Compute, Intelligence AI, and more.

Why you should join

  • This will be one of the highest signal, talent dense communities you will be a part of. We will recruit exceptional talent who have the highest potential, no matter what path they pursue (operating, founding, or investing).
  • This program aims to accelerate your path to your goals and maximize your opportunity set in your career.
  • You get proprietary access to internships, full-time opportunities at the best startups (e.g. Applied Compute, Mercor), exposure to some of the best founders, operators and investors, and also fun retreats in SF and NY with your cohort.
